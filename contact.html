About Job Connect

Experience Never Retires — Empowering Retired Professionals

Our Vision

Job Connect is an innovative platform designed to bridge the gap between retired professionals and employers who value experience. The idea is simple yet powerful — “Experience Never Retires.” Our mission is to provide opportunities for skilled retirees to stay active, share their expertise, and continue contributing to society while earning respect and income.

How It Works

Retired professionals can register for free, choose their category (like teaching, accounting, management, etc.), and explore available jobs tailored to their expertise. Employers can post openings and access experienced candidates for a small consultancy fee of ₹29. This makes Job Connect an easy, affordable, and efficient way to find trusted, skilled talent.

Our Story

The idea was born during the Viksit Bharat Buildathon 2025 — as a solution to real-life problems faced by retired citizens in India who wish to remain productive and financially independent. The project emphasizes inclusivity, dignity, and digital empowerment.
